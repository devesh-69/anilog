# Changelog

All notable changes to AniLog. Versions follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH.

## 1.1.2 — October 2026

Opens on your Library, with nicer anime pages.

- AniLog now opens on your Library
- Anime pages: a taller banner that fades into the page, and long titles no longer run into it
- Status bar icons stay readable over banners and on every theme
- Importing a backup no longer adds a "Completed" memory dated today for every anime

## 1.1.1 — October 2026

Big libraries feel instant again.

- Typing in Library search, filtering and editing no longer freeze the app with large libraries
- Search puts the anime you name first ("our dating story" finds Our Dating Story) and ignores far-off matches
- Anime that share the same title are no longer hidden from the Library
- Libraries with tens of thousands of anime load without errors

## 1.1.0 — October 2026

A fresh new look, a real timeline, and smoother backups.

- Redesigned every screen with a calmer, cleaner look that works in all 8 themes, light and dark
- Timeline is now a journal: a monthly recap, entries grouped by day, search, filters and pinned memories
- Write memories from anywhere: pick the anime, add an episode, moods, and keep them private or pinned
- Library: one Filters sheet for mood, tag and genre, a poster grid view, and easier multi-select
- Pick from my list: find something to rewatch or to recommend to a friend, or shows like one you pick, best-rated first with the reason why
- Smarter matching: more accurate moods and "Similar in your archive" that favours close matches and skips shows you dropped
- Rankings: a podium for your top three and an Edit mode to reorder or remove
- Stats: watch time, library by status, completions per month, your scores and personal records, for this year or all time
- Tags: pick a colour when you create one and see how many anime use each tag
- Search shows English titles when you type English, and Japanese or romaji titles when you type those
- Backups: .anilog files named by date and time, now including your timeline, with a step-by-step conflict review on import
- Web: timeline sync, a clear sync status, and more reliable syncing
- Tablets and desktop get a side navigation rail
- New "What's new" screen and About section in Settings

## 1.0.0 — July 2026

The first release of AniLog.

- Track anime with status, progress, ratings and watch dates
- Add anime by searching online, or manually
- Rewatch history, rankings and custom tags
- Statistics dashboard
- Export and import backups
- Theme presets
- Web version with an account and cloud sync
